💡 Important Setup Instructions:
  • Folder Location: In GitHub, place the 3 workflow YAML files inside .github/workflows/ in your repo. (In the ZIP, they are in both .github/workflows/ and github-workflows/).
  • R2 File Types:
    • eod_latest.json.gz: Daily latest trading day snapshot.
    • daily/YYYY-MM-DD.json.gz: Daily immutable date archives.
    • history_latest.json.gz: Yearly / Multi-Day full candle history for backtesting.
    • symbols.json: Master catalog covering 3,500+ NSE & BSE stocks.
  • Automatic Deploy: Pushing to main or master automatically deploys to GitHub Pages.
